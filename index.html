Methods

How to read the measures

Data

Posts from Anxiety, Depression, Lonely, MentalHealth, and SuicideWatch were aggregated by month across 2021. The analysis contains 578,454 posts with source coverage.

Activity

post_count measures how many posts appeared in each community and month. It does not measure engagement, sentiment, or distress.

Engagement

Reddit score is the original upvote-based post score. Average score is sensitive to a small number of high-score posts; median score describes the middle post.

Sentiment

Sentiment was computed from post text and aggregated monthly. It is an approximate measure of language and is not a clinical assessment of users or communities.

Language

TF-IDF was calculated after stopword, platform-noise, minimum-frequency, and minimum-document-frequency filtering. A high value indicates relative distinctiveness, not necessarily high overall frequency.

Missing data

Depression × May 2021 is unavailable in the final source datasets. The heatmap shows a patterned missing cell, the scatterplot omits the point, and the sentiment line shows a gap.

Looking at any one signal alone would miss important differences across communities.
